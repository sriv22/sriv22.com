The equity piece

Why the curve bends up instead of down

The initial $320,800 grant vests 35% / 28% / 22% / 15%, so on its own it declines every year: $112,280 → $89,824 → $70,576 → $48,120. Left alone, that front-loaded schedule would drag total comp down about $64K by year 4.

Refreshers flip it. Each annual refresher compounds 3% from the initial grant and vests on the same schedule, so by year 4 four grants are vesting at once:

Refresher grants: Apr 2027 — $330,424 · Apr 2028 — $340,337 · Apr 2029 — $350,547

Year 4 equity breakdown: $48,120 initial + $290,679 refreshers = $338,799

Bottom line

Growth depends almost entirely on refreshers continuing at roughly initial-grant size. If a refresher comes in smaller or skips a year, years 3–4 flatten fast — that is the number to watch each review cycle.

Market reality check

Grant value vs. what the shares are worth today

The grant was priced at Uber’s April 28, 2026 close of $74.11, about 4,329 shares. Uber closed October 26, 2026 at $66.13, down 29.7% over the past year, so the full grant is worth about $286,000 at today’s price, not $320,800 — and the year-1 vest would be worth about $100,200 instead of $112,280.

Open question on the projection: Uber’s stock trend (down ~30% over the past year) and its business growth (revenue up 16.7% year over year) point in opposite directions, so no stock growth rate is baked into the table above. Picking one changes the equity column.

Assumptions

Everything the model takes as given
  • Start date April 28, 2026; equity vests yearly at 35% / 28% / 22% / 15%.
  • Bonus at the midpoint ($52,500), held flat across all four years.
  • Base grows 3% a year (inflation).
  • One refresher grant each anniversary, compounding 3% a year from the initial grant, vesting on the same schedule.
  • $25,000 sign-on in year 1 only. No promotion, no bonus growth, no stock-price change in the table.

Figures are pre-tax and exclude benefits. Equity values in the table are grant values; realized value moves with Uber’s stock price.
