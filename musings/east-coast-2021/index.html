Earning our Leaf Peeper badge

In October 2021, we road tripped from Chicago, all the way to Maine, then down to North Carolina and back in search of some magnificent colors 🍂

The Itinerary

Chicago ➡️ Toronto

The first leg of the trip was an "easy" 9 hour drive to the Great North. Toronto was the cool pitstop city.

📍Toronto ➡️ Montreal

In Montreal, we lived like locals for a week, hitting up the local bakeries, working from our adorable airbnb near La Plateau, walking through parks, and just wandering around a bit aimlessly.

Montreal and Quebec are beautifully quaint, and randomly very french. PSA, cause we got bit by this, things are mostly closed on Monday #justQuebecThings
