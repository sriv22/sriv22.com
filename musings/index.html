After 29 something years, my mom got celebrate Vinakya Chavati with the family in India.

Vinakya Chavati is to honor Lord Ganesha, to remind ourselves that we can overcome any obstacle in our way, to celebrate with our families and all the ways we stand by each other, and to cherish the special moments and traditions together.

My grandma, uncles, aunts, cousins - even our little pupper Cady - all came together to take part in age old traditions like drying our hair with aromatic sambrani, gather ayurvedic plants, hand build puja items, and cook enough food to feed a village. It was a beautiful reminder that no matter the distance nor the time, traditions and family are the common thread that run through me, you, us - making us all whole
